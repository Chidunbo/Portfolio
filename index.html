AI Foundation: Hands-On AI Learning for Middle School

🏆 2026 Presidential AI Challenge State Champion

September 2025 - December 2025

How can middle school students learn about AI through a hands-on, designer-oriented approach? I designed and taught AI Foundation: Creativity, Code, and Consequences, a pilot elective for 7th–8th-grade students at Menaul School. In the final project, students guided a Sphero robot through an obstacle course using both block-based programming and a machine-learning hand-gesture controller. Comparing the two approaches helped students examine key AI tradeoffs, including flexibility versus precision, transparency versus black-box behavior, and the effects of biased training data.

Prism Pines: A Gender-Inclusive Learning Game

October 2022 - December 2023

Prism Pines is a 2D role-playing game designed with OUT Maine to help children ages 9–13 explore diverse gender identities through stories, characters, and play. As the research and art sub-team lead, I helped translate feedback from LGBTQ+ youth into the game’s learning goals, narrative, character interactions, and visual design.

Inclusive Classroom: Co-Designing an ASL Learning Program

February 2023 - May 2023

We explored how an immersive learning program could make ASL education more engaging and responsive to community needs. Through interviews with five professional ASL interpreters and research with educators, parents, and learners, our team developed the program through community research, ideation, co-design, and prototyping.

AP CSA Array Algorithm Visualizer

September 2025 - May 2026

While teaching AP CS A, I designed a website for my students to visualize and practice array-related algorithms.
